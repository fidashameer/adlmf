Real-harness policy comparison

Real operating-system processes against etcd 3.7.2. Unlike the simulator
experiments these runs are not seeded, so every difference has to survive
scheduling variance. Figures are mean +/- the half-width of a 95%
confidence interval. A difference is only called real where the intervals
do not overlap.

== Starvation (45 trials, 12 s each, 20 services over 3 locks) ==
worst-starved is the number of times the most-aborted service was
sacrificed in a run. It measures starvation directly, separately from
whether starvation costs any throughput.
